Final Year College Project & Architecture Specifications

Project Title: ResuCoach AI

Domain: Full-Stack Web Development, NLP, AI Coaching

A+ Evaluation Grade
1. Abstract & Problem Statement

Modern corporate recruiting utilizes Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter candidate resumes automatically. Over 75% of qualified student applicants are rejected due to missing technical keywords or unoptimized impact formatting. ResuCoach AI provides an end-to-end career platform combining automated ATS scoring, keyword gap detection, AI power bullet rewrites, live voice mock interviews, and CTC salary estimation.

2. Core Modules Implemented
  • ATS Resume Analyzer: 0-100 Score engine assessing Keyword Match %, Action Verbs, and Structure.
  • AI Job Matcher: Compares candidate resume against LinkedIn/Indeed job postings.
  • Live ATS Resume Builder: Real-time Harvard/Tech format builder with print PDF support.
  • AI Mock Interview Coach: Live Speech-to-Text microphone recording with AI answer evaluation.
  • Searchable Question Bank: Filterable database of technical and behavioral model answers.
  • Salary Estimator: CTC market estimation & offer negotiation script generator.
3. Technology Stack

HTML5, Tailwind CSS 3, JavaScript ES6, GSAP 3 (GreenSock Animations), Chart.js, Web Speech API, Node.js & Express REST APIs.
